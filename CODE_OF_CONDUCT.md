# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a friendly, welcoming and respectful community.

## Expected behaviour

- Be considerate and respectful in discussions.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the community and the project.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults or discriminatory remarks.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the project maintainers. Maintainers may remove
content or block contributors who violate this code of conduct.
